What is an Ijazah with Connected Sanad?

In Islamic scholarship, an Ijazah is not merely a degree—it is an authentic spiritual license granted after oral examination, certifying that the bearer recites the Holy Quran with total fidelity, precisely as transmitted through an unbroken succession of masters directly to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Riwayah Hafs ‘an ‘Asim
The universal recitation standard taught with exacting phonetic articulation (Makharij).
Warsh ‘an Nafi‘ & 10 Qira'at
Specialist tracks for advanced students seeking complete classical memorization mastery.
