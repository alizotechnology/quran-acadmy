Course Overview & Learning Objectives

The Tajweed Mastery & Ijazah Track is an elite, scholarly curriculum designed for students who possess proficient Quranic reading ability and aspire to elevate their recitation to the highest canonical perfection. Under the live 1-on-1 tutelage of Ijazah-certified scholars from Egypt and Medina, students analyze the foundational theoretical texts of Tajweed and execute a complete Khatm (oral recital) of the Holy Quran.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key inquiries regarding prerequisites, certification validity, and scheduling.

What is the prerequisite level needed to enroll in the Ijazah Track? expand_more

Students should already be capable of reading the Quranic Mushaf fluently without major pauses. You do not need to have memorized the entire Quran before beginning; we offer two tracks: Ijazah in Recitation with Looking (Tilawah wa Hifz Juz’i) and Ijazah in Memorization (Hifz). Our Shaykhs assess your exact level during the 30-minute free diagnostic evaluation.

Is the Ijazah certificate internationally recognized and authentic? expand_more

Yes. The Ijazah is the centuries-old gold standard of Islamic certification. Your document features an explicit, unbroken oral chain (Sanad Muttasil) listing every master scholar connecting you directly to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. It is signed, stamped with the teacher’s personalized seal, and authenticated by Al-Azhar or Medina faculty.

Can female students request qualified female scholars? expand_more

Absolutely. Al-Huda has an esteemed dedicated faculty of verified female Alimahs and Ijazah holders graduated from prestigious Egyptian and Saudi institutions. Sisters and young girls can choose female-only 1-on-1 instruction with total comfort and privacy.

What happens if I miss a lesson or need to reschedule? expand_more

We recognize the demanding schedules of modern families. You can reschedule or pause any session through your student portal or WhatsApp support line with at least 4 hours notice without losing your lesson credit.
