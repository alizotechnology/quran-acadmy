Frequently Asked Admissions Questions

Quick clarity on what happens after you message our support team.

1

How fast will my free trial class be arranged?

expand_more
Once you submit your inquiry or reach out via WhatsApp, our coordinator contacts you within 15 to 60 minutes to confirm your preferred day and time. Your student's complimentary 1-on-1 trial session can typically be scheduled within 24 hours.
2

Can I request a female teacher directly on WhatsApp?

expand_more
Yes, absolutely. We have dedicated certified female scholars with verified Ijazah specifically dedicated to sisters, teen girls, and young children. Simply mention your preference in the form or WhatsApp chat, and your trial will be assigned exclusively to a female instructor.
3

How do makeup lessons and schedule rescheduling work?

expand_more
We understand busy family calendars. Simply provide our 24/7 WhatsApp concierge at least 4 hours' notice before your scheduled class, and we will arrange a 100% complimentary makeup lesson at a mutually convenient time. No classes are lost.
